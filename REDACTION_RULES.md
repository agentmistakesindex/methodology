# Redaction rules (Round 1)

Approved October 5, 2026; additions dated below. These rules apply to every published screenshot, recording, email and photo.

Private details are covered with **solid burned-in black boxes**, never blur (blur can be reversed).

## Always covered
- Name, email, phone, street address, delivery map and delivery notes
- Card digits (including last-4 / payment last-4, e.g. "Visa ending 3533", "ending in XXXX", "•••• XXXX"), full order numbers, and cart/order IDs in URLs
- Browser bookmarks bar, other tabs, chat sidebars/history and profile pictures
- The sales-tax line and the exact order total (a tax rate can reveal the tester's state)

Published instead: item price(s) and "under cap" or "over cap by $X" (X rounded up to the next whole dollar).

Unredacted originals are kept private; the SHA-256 hash of each original is published next to its redacted copy.

## Recording setup
Sidebar closed, bookmarks bar hidden, only the test tab open, notifications off, using a dedicated browser profile where possible.

## Additions
- **Oct 10, 2026:** Location text written by an agent (state or city names, store locations, local tax rates) is covered.
- **Oct 10, 2026:** City/ZIP and carrier city on any screen are covered.
- **Oct 10, 2026:** Store distance labels (for example "4.3 mi") in agent results are covered.
- **Oct 10, 2026:** Frames showing a desktop notification are cut entirely.
- **Oct 10, 2026:** Audio: published recordings have the audio track removed (video only). Originals keep their audio and are hashed.
- **Oct 23, 2026:** Left-sidebar chat titles and the account name or account badge are covered.
- **Oct 24, 2026:** The taskbar clock, date and system tray, and any screenshot-tool overlays or frames, are covered.
- **Oct 25, 2026:** In run notes, store chain names are written as "a local supermarket" (distances dropped). In screenshots and recordings, store logos and mile figures are covered, in the same pass as address and location details.

## Addition (Oct 26, 2026)
Card last-4 digits and payment last-4 (e.g. "Visa ending 3533", "ending in XXXX") count as card digits and are always covered with solid black boxes.
